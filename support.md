<h1>Authors</h1>

Wayne Schlitt is the primary author of libspf2. The SPF protocol was
originally proposed and designed by Meng Wong. Contributing authors
(in approximate alphabetical order) include:

<ul>
<li>Dean Strik (postfix)</li>
<li>Eric Dorland (Debian support)</li>
<li>Jef Poskanzer (spfmilter)</li>
<li>Michel Bouissou (postfix and Mandrake support)</li>
<li>Michael Brumm (Win32 support)</li>
<li>Shevek (bugs and documentation)</li>
</ul>
